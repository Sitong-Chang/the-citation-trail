04 / CURATORIAL NOTE

READING THE RECORD

What travels
through an archive?

A private diary does not enter public memory whole.

This curation follows six excerpt groups from 《贞丰八年血泪录》 as they appear across seven public-facing sources: a local chronicle, an academic periodical, news outlets, an official archival platform, a local media platform, and a memorial inscription.

When the same fragments recur across different channels, they become easier to recognise, repeat, and treat as the central account. The network makes that repetition visible. Its dense connections do not, by themselves, prove that one institution directly copied another; they show that the same excerpt groups have been selected by multiple sources.

The curatorial question is therefore not only what has been preserved, but also what kinds of material become legible as public history. The diary's author should not be reduced to the fragments that circulate most often. The figure of a witness to wartime violence may be historically important, while still being only one dimension of a person whose everyday life is not represented by this network.

什么穿行于
档案之间？

一份私人日记进入公共记忆时，并不会以完整的面貌出现。

本次策展追踪《贞丰八年血泪录》的六组摘录在七个公共传播来源中的出现情况。样本包括地方志、学术期刊、新闻媒体、官方档案平台、地方融媒体平台与纪念性铭文。

当相同片段在不同渠道反复出现，它们就更容易被识别、转述，并逐渐被视为这份日记最核心的内容。网络图将这种重复呈现出来。但密集的连线本身并不能证明某一机构直接复制了另一机构；它只能说明多个来源选择了相同的摘录组。

因此，策展所追问的不只是哪些内容被保存下来，还有哪些内容更容易成为公共历史。作者不应被压缩为传播最广的几个片段。作为战争暴行见证者的形象具有重要历史意义，但它仍然只是一个人的某个维度；这张网络并不能代表其未被纳入传播的日常生活。

05 / METHOD & LIMITS

What the network can — and cannot — say

Each source is associated with the excerpt IDs listed in the project's working dataset. An undirected edge is drawn when two sources share one or more excerpt IDs. Edge thickness represents the count of shared IDs. Node size represents the number of excerpt groups associated with that source.

This is a map of excerpt overlap, not a verified citation genealogy. It does not establish who quoted whom, whether two sources consulted the same intermediate publication, or whether wording was edited between appearances. Those claims require source-by-source comparison of publication dates, footnotes, bibliographies, and exact textual variants.

Transcription and attribution note: the excerpt texts and source associations in this prototype were carried over from the project's existing working draft. They are not presented as independently authenticated transcriptions. Check each passage against the cited publication or inscription before public release.
